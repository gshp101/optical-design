Notes

  • Match is the worst grade among the first-order quantities EFL, BFL, GIH and VL. Exact means within the precision the book prints (one unit of the last digit, or 0.05 % where the book's rounded field angle limits GIH), Close within 0.5 %, Off anything larger.
  • Model values are paraxial at the primary wavelength (d line). NA is 1/(2 × working F/#), GIH is the paraxial image height at the largest field, VL is the sum of all thicknesses except the last, and PTZ/F is −1/(EFL Σ c(n′−n)/(n n′)). Where the image lies inside an immersion medium of index n′ (Chapter 15), the book's EFL is n′ times the focal length, and the model's EFL is given the same way.
  • NA reflects the entrance pupil entered in the model rather than the prescription, so it is shown but not graded. Chapter 6 models use EPD = 100/F# from the book's rounded F/#, which puts NA a few tenths of a percent from the book's value; later chapters use EPD = 2 EFL |NA|, or for a finite object the EPD that gives the book's working NA.
  • PTZ/F is shown for reference but not graded into Match. Where it differs by a few percent while EFL, BFL and VL agree, the difference is most likely in how the book's software defines it rather than in the prescription.
  • Rays: a full axial beam and the chief rays at 0.7 and 1.0 field are traced at every wavelength. Vignetting at full field is reported when the book's semi-apertures clip the beam. Models with a half field of 60° or more use real ray aiming.
  • Models not graded Exact: Fig 13.6 (BFL), Fig 13.7 (BFL, GIH), Fig 13.10 (BFL), Fig 13.14 (EFL, BFL, GIH). These use glasses entered from the book's rounded nd/Vd or a modern substitute with slightly different dispersion (see the glass column).
  • PTZ/F differs by more than 0.5 % on Fig 13.6, 13.10, 13.11, 13.14.
