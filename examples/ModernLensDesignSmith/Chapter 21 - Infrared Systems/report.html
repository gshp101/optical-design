Notes

  • Match is the worst grade among the first-order quantities EFL, BFL, GIH and VL. Exact means within the precision the book prints (one unit of the last digit, or 0.05 % where the book's rounded field angle limits GIH), Close within 0.5 % (for BFL, 0.5 % of the EFL when that is larger), Off anything larger.
  • Model values are paraxial at the primary wavelength (the d line unless the model notes give other wavelengths). NA is 1/(2 × working F/#), GIH is the paraxial image height at the largest field, VL is the sum of all thicknesses except the last, and PTZ/F is −1/(EFL Σ c(n′−n)/(n n′)). Where the image lies inside an immersion medium of index n′ (Chapter 15), the book's EFL is n′ times the focal length, and the model's EFL is given the same way. After an odd number of mirrors (Chapter 16) light travels backwards and n′ is negative; the book prints EFL and BFL with the sign they would have unfolded, and so does the model.
  • NA reflects the entrance pupil entered in the model rather than the prescription, so it is shown but not graded. Chapter 6 models use EPD = 100/F# from the book's rounded F/#, which puts NA a few tenths of a percent from the book's value; later chapters use EPD = 2 EFL |NA|, or for a finite object the EPD that gives the book's working NA.
  • PTZ/F is shown for reference but not graded into Match. Where it differs by a few percent while EFL, BFL and VL agree, the difference is most likely in how the book's software defines it rather than in the prescription.
  • Rays: a full axial beam and the chief rays at 0.7 and 1.0 field are traced at every wavelength. Vignetting at full field is reported when the book's semi-apertures clip the beam. Models with a half field of 60° or more use real ray aiming.
  • Models not graded Exact: Fig 21.4 (BFL), Fig 21.5 (BFL), Fig 21.6 (BFL, GIH), Fig 21.7 (EFL, BFL, GIH), Fig 21.9 (BFL), Fig 21.10 (BFL), Fig 21.11 (BFL). These use glasses entered from the book's rounded nd/Vd or a modern substitute with slightly different dispersion (see the glass column).
  • Discrepancies with the book (the model is entered as printed):
    • Fig 21.12: With germanium at 4.00348 the EFL matches, but the paraxial image lies about 998 behind the ideal lens instead of the book's 1026.
    • Fig 21.13: With germanium at 4.00323 the EFL matches, but the paraxial image lies about 1000 behind the ideal lens instead of the book's 1032.
    • Fig 21.14: The telescope is so nearly afocal that the EFL and image distance behind the ideal lens change with the fourth decimal of the germanium index; with germanium at 4.00333 the EFL matches, but the paraxial image lies about 998 behind the ideal lens instead of the book's 1024, so the image is left at the printed 1000.
  • The book's VL leaves out an air space on Fig 21.12, 21.13, 21.14, so it is shown but not graded into Match (see the notes in the model file).
  • PTZ/F differs by more than 0.5 % on Fig 21.5, 21.7, 21.11, 21.12.
